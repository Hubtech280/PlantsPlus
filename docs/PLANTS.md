# Plant mechanics

## Lotus Pumpkin — 6000

Pumpkin + Snow Lotus. A defensive fusion with a five-charge heal for the protected plant.

## Bambnut — 6001

Bamblock + Wall-nut. A defensive Snow/Day cross fusion.

## Magnet-o-pea — 6002

Peashooter + Magnet-shroom. Absorbs a supported metal item placed on it and changes its projectile mode.

## Iceberg-shroom — 6003

Ice-shroom + Ice-shroom. Deals 40 damage and freezes for 8 seconds. Freeze-immune enemies are slowed instead.

## Witchfire Pumpkin — 6004

Pyro Pumpkin + Doom Pumpkin. Weak Odyssey plant that buffs and heals its protected plant while unlit, then sacrifices protected plants and triggers Doom/Jalapeno effects while lit.

## Nutty Sharpshooter — 6005

Spruce Sharpshooter + Wall-nut. A 4000-toughness shooter with piercing and knockback.

## Inferno Torchflower — 6006

Infernowood + Sunflower. Lit projectiles add 50 energy, up to 250. Clicking releases all stored Sun with a multiplier of 0.5 per charge and resets the reserve and energy.

## Pumpkin Podbomber — 6007

Explode-o-shooter + Pumpkin. Copies a protected Pea-family plant and periodically replaces volleys with Explode-o-peas.

## Ceasarweed — 6008

Salad-pult + Spikeweed. Stores missed lobbed shots to fire extra salads, up to 10, and applies Butter.

## Solar Firnace — 6009

Firnace absorbs the Sunflower below it. A flying timed plant that produces Sun, can extend its lifetime and protects the plant below from cold.

## Not-a-pea — 6010

Saw-me-not > Peashooter. Fires piercing saw-peas. Every hit has a 25% chance to attach a saw that deals periodic damage for 10 seconds.

## Not-a-storm Commando — 6011

Saw-me-not > Pea-storm Commando. A Strong Odyssey fusion using the complete Pea-storm volley with piercing, attachable saw-peas.

## Frost Furflower — 6012

Hoarfrost Lichen > Sunflower. Produces 25 Sun normally. A direct snowball hit drops 100 Sun and freezes other freezable plants in the surrounding 3×3 area.

## Doomtronion — 6013

Amp-nion > Doom-shroom. Connected Amp-nions and Doomtronions increase its damage. The shot chains to up to three zombies, applies Irradiation and may trigger a craterless Doom explosion.

## Lichen-pea — 6014

Hoarfrost Lichen > Peashooter. Fires slowing peas. Hits can freeze two to four nearby freezable plants.

## Logic Blover — 6015

Harvest-exclusive Red Card. Pushes zombies back, gives each one a random elemental effect, improves Gift Box luck while on the lawn and can blow again after receiving a Gold Bean for 10,000 money.

## Solar Sharpshooter — 6016

Spruce Sharpshooter > Sunflower. Fires a piercing projectile and drops 25 Sun for every zombie actually hit.

## Sea Ballista — 6017

Spruce Ballista > Sea-shroom. An aquatic two-tile plant with 3.5-tile range. Its bolts pierce twice, explode after impact and knock zombies back.

## Pineshooter — 6018

Peashooter > Spruce Sharpshooter. Fires pine trees that knock back the first zombie. A collision with another zombie damages and briefly stuns both.

## Icytronion — 6019

Amp-nion > Ice-shroom. Connected electric-family plants increase its damage. Its chain shot slows every target and has a 25% chance to freeze.
